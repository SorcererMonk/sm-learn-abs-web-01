# Motion Design (Full Pipeline) → Training Programme
## T8: Design: 19 · Storyboard Sprints · Styleframe Sets · Three-Pass Animation · Kinetic Type · Compositing · Sound Sync · Delivery · Showreel · Taste Forge

> This is the **training** counterpart to `L8-design-19-motion-full-pipeline.md`. The L file is the class: the discipline, pre-production, designing for motion, animation craft, compositing and 3D, sound and edit, delivery, and practice. This file is the room afterwards. Nothing here is new material. Everything is repetition, pressure and production, aimed at:
>
> - **Muscle memory:** turning a brief into a one-line idea, thumbnails, styleframes, an animatic and a delivered master; blocking, splining and polishing without skipping passes; exporting correct specs for every platform.
> - **Intuition:** reading a graph-editor curve as motion, feeling when a cut is two frames off the beat, spotting a floaty or mechanical move instantly, and knowing when a piece needs less.
> - **Taste:** your own motion voice (graphic, cinematic, playful, restrained), built through short, frequent, finished pieces rather than stalled epics.
>
> Reference: `L#` means that Part of the L file. **Tools (your free path first):** paper and a pen for thumbnails; **Figma** for styleframes *[Figma: Yellow]*; **Cavalry** (free since April 2026) for 2D motion; **DaVinci Resolve** (free) for edit, Fusion compositing, grade and audio; **Blender** (free) for 3D and Grease Pencil; **After Effects** if you subscribe or trial. Click-level recipes are in `T8-design-38-after-effects.md` (with Cavalry and Resolve steps) and `T8-design-43-lottie-rive.md`.

## 1. How to Train

| Shape | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Drill** | 15–30 min | Section 3. Curves, timing, type, transitions, delivery reps. |
| **Sprint** | 60–120 min | Section 4. One pre-production or animation deliverable. |
| **Piece** | 3–10 h over a week | Section 5. A finished, delivered short piece. |
| **Trial** | 30–45 min | Section 6. A flawed piece, a timer, a diagnosis. |
| **Study** | 45–90 min | Section 7. Frame-by-frame breakdown and a rebuild of a master's shot. |
| **Forge** | 30 min, weekly | Section 9. Style rules, templates, reel. |

**Rules of the room:**
1. **No new theory during T time.**
2. **Every week ends with a finished render,** however short: a 5–15 second piece, exported, posted privately or publicly, and logged in `design-training/19-motion/LOG.md`.
3. **Paper first:** a one-line idea and thumbnails before opening any software. No thumbnails, no piece.
4. **Styleframe before animation** for anything longer than 5 seconds.
5. **Three passes, saved separately:** blocking, spline, polish renders kept for comparison.
6. **Sound from the animatic onward.** Silent animation is only allowed in D2 drills.
7. **Deliver to spec:** every piece exported in at least two formats (e.g. 16:9 and 9:16) with safe zones checked.
8. **Log every failure** (Section 12) by mechanism: no idea, unreadable type, floaty curves, mechanical motion, no holds, off-beat cut, over-finishing, bad safe zone, wrong spec, unlicensed asset.

**The weekly minimum:** 1 finished piece (5–15 s) · 3 drills · 1 sprint · 1 trial or study · 1 forge entry · 20 minutes of watching (Art of the Title, Motionographer) with notes.

## 3. Drill Bank

**D1 — Curve reps (any tool)**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|
| Bouncing ball in three passes | 20 min |
| Same move with 5 speed-graph shapes; label the feel of each | 15 min |
| Separate-dimension arc (X and Y curves) for a thrown object | 10 min |
| Overshoot and settle on a scale-in, two frames of overlap on a child layer | 10 min |

**D2 — Timing and spacing (silent)**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|
| Move–hold–move rhythm on 3 shapes | 10 min |
| Heavy vs light object falling (spacing only) | 10 min |
| Stagger 8 letters with 2-frame offsets, then 4-frame; compare | 10 min |
| Timing chart on paper for a 24-frame move with ease-out | 5 min |

**D3 — Type reps**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|
| 8-word line revealed word by word with holds at ~3 words/s | 15 min |
| Variable-font weight pulse on a word synced to a beat | 15 min |

| Readability check of 3 type treatments on a phone at arm's length | 5 min |

**D4 — Transition reps**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|

**D5 — Pre-production reps**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|

| Colour script of 8 frames | 15 min |

**D6 — Delivery reps**
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|
| Export 16:9 H.264 and 9:16 H.264 with safe-zone check | 10 min |
| Transparent ProRes 4444 export of a lower third | 10 min |
| Loudness check and normalise a mix to −14 LUFS | 10 min |

**D8 — Genre, camera and procedural reps** (L# 8–10)
| Drill | Target |
|---|---|
| Time the beats of a 60 s explainer (hook, problem, solution, how, proof, CTA) | 6 min |
| Name the camera move and its meaning in 5 film clips (pan, dolly, truck, zoom, rack focus) | 5 min |
| 2.5D push-in on 4 layers with correct parallax and scale compensation | 10 min |

**D7 — Recall (Anki)**
The pipeline steps, what each pre-production artefact locks, ~130–150 wpm VO, ~3 words/s reading, frame rates, resolutions and ratios, codecs and when to use them, loudness targets, the three passes, graph types, title-design history dates (Bass 1955/1958/1959, Binder 1962, Cooper 1995, Elastic 2011). **Target: 95%, 10 min daily.**

## 4. Sprint Bank

**Level 1 — Concept and pre-production**
1. **Explainer reverse-engineering** (L# 1 analysis): script transcribed, key frames captured, beat timecodes logged. *Accept:* wpm and shot count calculated.
2. **Three directions** from one brief (a treatment paragraph + 1 styleframe each) (`TD-02#49`). *Accept:* a friend can tell the three apart instantly.
3. **Animatic** for a 30-second VO. *Accept:* timing locked, reviewed by one person, notes applied.

**Level 2 — Design for motion**
4. **Styleframe set** of 4 frames at final quality with layer-separated source files. *Accept:* each works as a poster; every moving element is its own layer.
5. **Colour script** vs a film sequence (L# 2 analysis) (`TD-14#5`, `#14`). *Accept:* 8 frames with an emotional arc written under each.
6. **Constraint study** (`TD-08#3`): one subject, three colours, one thesis, 7 seconds. *Accept:* finished and exported.

**Level 3 — Animation craft**
7. **Three-pass trilogy** (L# 3 analysis): ball, logo reveal, 10-word kinetic line, each with pass renders. *Accept:* side-by-side comparison with notes on what polish added.
8. **Logo animation** of a Phase 3 identity (10) in 3 seconds, telling the mark's idea. *Accept:* not a spin; the construction or meaning animates.
9. **Rig and reuse:** a simple character or object rig (parenting, nulls, IK or puppet pins) used in two different shots.

**Level 4 — Compositing and sound**
10. **Blender + composite** (L# 4 analysis; `TD-08#15`). *Accept:* all passes listed; shadows and grade match.
11. **Re-cut to two tracks** (L# 5 analysis). *Accept:* key moves retimed to each; a note on how meaning changed.
12. **Sound design pass:** a silent 15 s animation given whooshes, hits, ambience and a mix. *Accept:* −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, checked on phone speakers.

**Level 5 — Delivery and practice**
13. **Four-format delivery** (L# 6 analysis). *Accept:* each checked on its platform/device.
14. **EcoTrace launch proposal** (L# 7 analysis). *Accept:* treatment, styleframe plan, schedule, price structure, revision rounds, rights.
15. **Title study** (L# 12 analysis): the first 10 seconds of a Saul Bass title rebuilt as a private study. *Accept:* a page on the graphic idea and timing.

**Level 6 — Genres, camera, systems and direction**
16. **Genre dossier** (L# 8). One genre per week for five weeks: five examples timed beat by beat, one page on the shared structure and the one example that breaks it well. *Accept:* five dossiers; each ends with a rule you'll use.
17. **Explainer beat sheet for EcoTrace** (L# 8, L# 1). The worked example's shot list extended to all 45 seconds, with a 100-word VO. *Accept:* read aloud in 45 s ± 2 s; each shot has one job.
18. **Lens study** (L# 9). The same 2.5D scene at 24 mm and 85 mm, then with and without motion blur. *Accept:* a note on how mood and speed changed, with frames.
19. **Procedural vs keyframed** (L# 10). The 100-circle ripple built both ways, then changed to 400 circles. *Accept:* time for each build and each change logged; a one-paragraph verdict.
20. **Data-driven video** (L# 10). A 10-second bar chart race of EcoTrace data from a CSV in Remotion or Cavalry. *Accept:* changing the CSV re-renders a correct video with no manual edits.
21. **Audio-reactive loop** (L# 10). 15 seconds where the kick drum drives scale and hi-hats drive small details. *Accept:* no jitter; the mapping is visible to a viewer who's told nothing.
22. **Director's round** (L# 11). Give and receive one round of timecoded notes on a 20-second piece, categorised as idea, timing, design or technical. *Accept:* notes prioritised; a changelog; a v2 render.
23. **Style guide** (L# 11) for a four-episode explainer series: palette, type, curves, durations, camera rules, textures, render settings. *Accept:* a second person could animate an episode that matches.

## 6. Critique & Failure Drills

- **Floaty/mechanical trial.** Five shots with planted curve problems (all easy-ease, linear moves, no overlap, no holds). Diagnose from the graph editor and fix in 30 minutes.
- **Readability trial.** A kinetic-type piece viewed on a phone at arm's length. Every line you can't read in time is a failure; retime or resize.
- **Over-finish trial.** A piece drowning in glow, grain and chromatic aberration. Strip it back to the minimum that still unifies it.
- **Beat trial.** A cut that feels wrong. Move cuts and hits in 1–2 frame steps against the waveform until they land; log the offset.
- **Spec trial.** A delivery package with planted errors (wrong frame rate, logo under the Reels UI, no alpha on an overlay, clipping audio, missing captions). Fix them all.
- **Hook trial.** Ten social openings; predict which hold attention past 3 seconds, then re-cut the three weakest so the first frame is the strongest.
- **Camera trial.** A piece with motivated and unmotivated camera moves mixed together. Remove every move that doesn't reveal, relate or emphasise, and compare.
- **Notes trial.** A thread of 12 conflicting stakeholder comments. Consolidate them into one prioritised, timecoded round and flag which break an earlier approval.
- **Live review.** For styleframes and boards, copy a Figma link into a Claude chat: *"Live review for T8-19 [Belt]. Check the idea's clarity, composition across 16:9 and 9:16 with safe zones, type readability at phone size, colour script consistency and layer preparation for animation."* For animation, upload an MP4 (and a screenshot of your graph editor for key shots) and ask for a critique of timing, curves, holds, transitions, sound sync and delivery. Log findings by mechanism.

**Postmortem template:** shot · failure · mechanism (idea, readability, curve, spacing, overlap, holds, cut, finish, spec, rights) · evidence (render, curve screenshot, phone test) · why missed · fix · the check that prevents it.

## 7. Study & Reproduce

**Weekly cycle:** pick a shot (a title sequence moment, a Buck or Oddfellows sequence, a Ben Marriott tutorial result) → watch at quarter speed → **predict** the keyframes, curves and layer structure → rebuild the shot as a private study → **diff** side by side at the same frame rate → one paragraph on the key decision (a hold, an overlap, a transition idea).

**Target list for the first year:** *Vertigo* titles (Bass) · *Anatomy of a Murder* titles · the Bond gun-barrel (Binder) · *Se7en* titles (Cooper) · *Game of Thrones* titles (Elastic) · a Buck brand film · an Oddfellows explainer · a ManvsMachine product film · a Ben Marriott "shape animation" tutorial result · a Jorge Canedo 2D sequence · a GMUNK or Ash Thorp UI sequence · a *Spider-Verse* colour sequence (`TD-14#5`) · a Studio Eeksaurus film · a Beeple Everyday · a *Vertigo* dolly zoom moment · a Zach Lieberman generative sketch · a bar chart race from a news site · a channel ident system (all variants of one idea).

## 8. Intuition Probes

- **Curve probes:** watch a move and sketch its speed graph before opening the file. Compare.
- **Timing probes:** guess a shot's duration in frames. Target error ≤ 4 frames at 24 fps.
- **Readability probes:** predict which lines viewers will miss. Test with 3 people on a phone.
- **Beat probes:** predict whether a cut is early, late or on the beat before checking the waveform.
- **Engagement probes:** for social posts, predict retention at 3 s and completion rate; check analytics.

- **Lens probes:** guess the focal length of a shot (wide, normal, long) before checking the metadata or a behind-the-scenes source.
- **Audience probes:** before sharing a piece, predict the one thing viewers will remember. Ask three viewers what they remember and compare.

Track hit rates in `LOG.md`. The target is ≥ 70% over the last 30 probes.

## 9. Taste Forge

- **Style rules.** Your motion voice as 5–7 rules (e.g. "hard ease-outs, soft landings", "one accent colour per scene", "type always leads"). Revised after every finished piece.
- **Templates.** Project folder template, naming convention, render presets (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, ProRes 4444, web loop), a styleframe template with safe-zone guides, an animatic template.
- **Asset library.** Your reusable rigs, transitions, sound effects (licensed), type animators and texture overlays. Solve the library problem once (`TD-08#1 · @juli_fella · DdBTl8XoPOE`).
- **Watching journal.** One piece per week with its idea, one technique, and one thing you'd steal.
- **Three ways.** One 5-second moment animated three ways (graphic, cinematic 3D, hand-drawn). Keep one, write why.
- **Decision journal.** For each finished piece: the one thing viewers should remember, the genre rule you followed or broke, the camera and tool decisions and why, and what viewers actually remembered. Patterns across ten entries become your style rules.
- **Procedural kit.** Your saved Cavalry setups, expression rigs and Remotion templates, each with a note on when it beats keyframing.
- **Constraint runs.** A piece with only squares. A piece with no colour. A 6-second piece that makes someone laugh. A piece with one cut. A piece where sound comes first and picture follows.

## 10. Assessment & Graduation

You are **trained in the motion pipeline** when all are true, with artefacts:

1. **Fluency:** D1–D6 within target; D7 recall at 95%.
2. **Volume:** 24 finished short pieces over six months.
3. **Pre-production:** three projects with treatments, storyboards, styleframes and animatics approved before animation.
4. **Craft:** three pieces with saved pass renders showing clear polish gains, and curves you can explain.
5. **Sound:** three pieces mixed to spec and checked on phone speakers.
6. **Delivery:** every piece delivered in at least two correct formats with safe zones and captions.
7. **Portfolio:** a 60-second showreel and three case-study pages with breakdowns.
8. **Range and direction:** pieces in three different genres (L# 8); one procedural or data-driven piece (L# 10); one project directed through a full feedback round with a written style guide (L# 11).
9. **Audience:** three pieces tested with viewers, with what they remembered recorded against what you intended.
10. **Taste and teaching:** style rules revised ≥ 3 times; you can teach the three passes and the graph editor in 15 minutes each.
