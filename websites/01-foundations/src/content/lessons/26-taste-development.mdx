## What good looks like

Semantic HTML that **reads well unstyled**; a small, explicit cascade (layers, low specificity, tokens); layouts that respond to their space and **survive any content, language or zoom level**; typography with restraint and generous spacing; colour defined perceptually and used sparingly; motion that is fast, meaningful and optional; **native elements styled beautifully** instead of divs rebuilt badly; components whose every state is designed; pages that are fast on a mid-range phone and **work without JavaScript**; and a visual voice that is recognisably yours without being loud.

## Deliberate exercises

- **Copywork**: recreate one excellent interface a week from a screenshot, without viewing its source, then compare with the real CSS.
- **Constraint studies**: the same page with one font size and weight only, greyscale only, no borders, no images, no JavaScript. Constraints reveal what actually creates hierarchy.
- **Read the CSS of sites you admire** in DevTools: tokens, layers, layout primitives, easings.
- **Unstyled review**: turn CSS off on your own pages monthly and read them.
- **Resize-and-break sessions**: 280px to 2560px, 400% zoom, RTL; fix what breaks.
- **One new CSS feature a week**, used in a real demo, with its Baseline status and fallback written down.
- **Design critique journal**: one screen a day, three sentences on what works and what doesn't.
- **Swipe file**: interfaces, type, palettes and motion you love, with notes on *why*.

## Anti-taste

`div` soup · buttons that are links and links that are buttons · `!important` as conflict resolution · IDs for styling · deep nesting and 0-4-0 specificity · magic numbers (`top: 37px`) · fixed heights on text containers · `px` font sizes that ignore user settings · `user-scalable=no` · removing focus outlines without replacements · placeholder as label · animating layout properties · `transition: all` · parallax and scroll-jacking by default · carousels nobody asked for · five fonts and nine weights · pure-black shadows and pure-grey neutrals · colour as the only signal · CSS frameworks nobody on the team can read without the docs · dark mode by `filter: invert()` · lorem ipsum in "final" designs · decorative motion that ignores reduced motion · copying a trendy aesthetic without the judgement that made the original work.
